What Argus Tech does

Remote PC care

We help with everyday Windows problems on computers that have the Argus app installed and are switched on and connected to the internet.

Fault reports

When something goes wrong, the app records the fault so it can be reviewed and handled in a clear, repeatable way.

Automatic fixes

Argus Pro Care plans include automatic fixes for covered problems, within the monthly allowance. Other work is done after you approve it.

Monthly plans or once-off help

Choose Argus Home Care, Argus Business Care or Argus Pro Care, or pay per fix if you are not on a plan.
