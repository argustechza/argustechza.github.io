Delivery policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Argus Tech services are delivered digitally and remotely. There are no physical goods and no shipping.

When service starts

After payment is received, service can begin as soon as the Argus app is installed on the computer and that computer is switched on and connected to the internet.

  • Monthly plans: coverage for the paid month starts when payment is received. Fault reports and included fixes can then be handled while the computer is reachable.
  • Argus Pro Care: automatic fixes included in the plan can run after payment, within the monthly allowance, when the computer is reachable.
  • Once-off fixes: work starts after you approve the charge and payment is received (or, for billed business work, after you approve the item), when the computer is reachable.

If a computer is switched off or offline, we cannot deliver the remote service until it is back online. Installing the Argus app itself does not cost extra.

No physical delivery

We do not post hardware, discs or printed materials. Everything is provided through the app and remote care.
