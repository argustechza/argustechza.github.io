Hardware limitations. Argus fixes software and Windows problems remotely. If Argus finds that a problem is caused by hardware — for example a failing disk, RAM, battery, overheating, or a PC that is simply too old or slow for what it is asked to do — Argus will tell you it is a hardware issue. Argus cannot fix hardware limitations or physical faults remotely. If a once-off fix ends in a hardware diagnosis and Argus cannot complete the work, you are not charged for that attempt. On a monthly plan, a hardware diagnosis does not use one of your included monthly fixes.

Fixes included in a monthly plan reset on the 1st of each month and do not carry over. When a PC reaches its monthly allowance, automatic fixes pause until the next reset. Extra fixes are charged at the once-off prices and only go ahead after you approve them. A check that finds nothing to fix does not use an allowance.
