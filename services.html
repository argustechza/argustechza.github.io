Services and pricing

Argus Tech provides remote PC care and support for businesses. Prices below are in ZAR. Payments are processed by PayFast. Argus Tech never stores card details. You can pay by card or EFT through PayFast.

Argus Business Care

Argus Business Care

R323 per PC / month

A dedicated support assistant looks after all your company’s PCs. Includes 20 remote fixes per PC each month. Staff home PCs can be added at the same price. Unlimited fixes for the whole company are quoted separately — contact us for a price.

Hardware limitations. Argus fixes software and Windows problems remotely. If Argus finds that a problem is caused by hardware — for example a failing disk, RAM, battery, overheating, or a PC that is simply too old or slow for what it is asked to do — Argus will tell you it is a hardware issue. Argus cannot fix hardware limitations or physical faults remotely. On Argus Business Care, a hardware diagnosis is simply a report.

Unused fixes reset on the 1st of each month and do not carry over. If a PC uses all 20 fixes in a month, further fixes wait until we approve them. Unlimited fixes for the whole company are quoted separately — contact us for a price.
