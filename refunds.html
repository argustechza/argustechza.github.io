Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Monthly plans

You may cancel Argus Business Care, or any other Argus monthly plan you already have, at any time by emailing argustch@gmail.com. Cancellation stops further charges from the next billing date. The current paid month is not refunded, because care is available for that month once payment has been received. Unused monthly fixes have no cash value and are not refunded, because they reset on the 1st of each month and do not carry over.

Hardware diagnosis

If Argus finds that a problem is caused by hardware and cannot fix it remotely, that is a report only. On Argus Business Care there is no extra charge for that diagnosis. If you are on an existing plan that includes a monthly fix allowance, a hardware diagnosis does not use one of those fixes, because no software fix could be completed.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are processed through PayFast back to the original payment method where PayFast allows it, or by EFT if that is how you paid. Please allow a few working days after we confirm the refund.

Quoted work

Bigger jobs are quoted before work starts. A quote is only charged if you accept it. If you decline, there is no charge for that quoted job.

Argus Tech

Remote PC care and support. Payments are processed by PayFast. Argus Tech never stores card details.
