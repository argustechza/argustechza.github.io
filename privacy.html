Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This short policy explains how Argus Tech handles personal information. It is written with POPIA in mind.

Information we use

We may use your name, email address, payment references from PayFast, and information about the computer that is needed to deliver care, such as the device name, fault reports and fix history. PayFast processes card and EFT payments. Argus Tech never stores card details.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide remote PC care, to bill for plans and fixes, to send service messages, and to keep records of work that was approved and carried out.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only where needed to provide the service, for example with PayFast for payments, or if the law requires it.

This website

This website is a static site. It does not use tracking cookies or analytics scripts.

Your requests

You may ask us what information we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we no longer need it for the service or for records we must keep. Email fn.nology@gmail.com.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it to provide the service and to maintain billing and work records.
