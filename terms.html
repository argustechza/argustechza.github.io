2. The service

We deliver care remotely through the Argus app. The app can report faults and apply fixes. Argus Pro Care plans include automatic fixes within the monthly allowance. 3. What you need to provide

Remote care can only be given when the Argus app is installed on the computer, the computer is switched on, and it is connected to the internet. You must have the right to allow care on that computer.

4. Plans, allowances and extra work
