1. Who we are

Argus Tech provides remote PC care and support. The plan we offer to new customers is Argus Business Care. 2. The service

We deliver care remotely through the Argus app. The app can report faults and apply fixes. Remote fixes included in the monthly allowance go ahead as part of the plan. We do not promise that every problem can be fixed. Bigger or unusual jobs are quoted first.

3. Hardware limitations

Argus fixes software and Windows problems remotely. If Argus finds that a problem is caused by hardware (for example a failing disk, RAM, battery, overheating, or a PC that is simply too old or slow for what it is asked to do), Argus will tell you it is a hardware issue. Argus cannot fix hardware limitations or physical faults remotely. Charges for a hardware diagnosis are explained in the refund and cancellation policy.

4. What you need to provide

Remote care can only be given when the Argus app is installed on the computer, the computer is switched on, and it is connected to the internet. You must have the right to allow care on that computer.

5. The plan and extra work

The price for Argus Business Care is listed on the services and pricing page. Argus Business Care is sold per PC and includes 20 remote fixes per PC each month. Staff home PCs can be added at the same price. Unused fixes reset on the 1st of each month and do not carry over. If a PC uses all 20 fixes in a month, further fixes wait until we approve them. Unlimited fixes for the whole company are available on request and are quoted separately.
